Build and deploy a polished, production-quality one-page marketing website for a new company called **ConsultRelay**.

## Context

ConsultRelay is validating a very narrow B2B managed service for U.S. dental marketing agencies and implant-focused dental practices.

The service is called:

**30-Day Implant Lead-to-Show Pilot**

The problem:

Dental marketing agencies generate expensive implant/full-arch leads for practices. Once a lead reaches the practice, response time, follow-up, scheduling, reminders and no-show recovery are often outside the agency's control.

ConsultRelay manages and measures that post-lead workflow through **attended implant consultation**.

We are NOT selling:

* an AI receptionist;
* a CRM;
* dental marketing;
* advertising;
* a phone system;
* clinical advice.

We integrate with the existing workflow and manage one measurable funnel:

**Paid Lead → Engaged → Qualified → Booked → Attended**

Clinical questions and treatment decisions always remain with clinic staff.

The initial pilot costs:

**$1,500 fixed for 30 days + direct communication costs**

Scope:

* one implant-focused dental practice;
* up to 100 newly opted-in paid implant leads;
* follow-up;
* non-clinical qualification;
* consultation booking;
* reminders;
* rescheduling/no-show recovery;
* human escalation;
* weekly lead-to-show reporting.

The website is primarily being visited by founders/CEOs/COOs/client-success leaders at U.S. dental marketing agencies after receiving a personalized cold email.

The website therefore needs to create trust extremely quickly.

# FAQ

## Is ConsultRelay an AI receptionist?

No. Existing tools already automate conversations and appointment scheduling. ConsultRelay focuses on operating and measuring one specific funnel: **paid implant lead → attended consultation**.

## Do you replace our CRM or HighLevel?

No. We prefer to work with the systems already in place rather than introduce another system of record.

## Does ConsultRelay replace the front desk?

No. Clinic staff continue to handle clinical questions, treatment decisions and situations requiring human judgment.

## Can you guarantee more implant cases?

No. We guarantee the defined workflow and measurement during the pilot. Clinical eligibility, patient decisions and treatment acceptance remain outside our control.

## What happens with clinical questions?

They are escalated to the clinic. ConsultRelay does not diagnose, determine implant suitability or recommend treatment.

## How is healthcare data handled?

Before a live pilot, the exact data flow, vendors, consent process, access controls and required agreements are documented with the participating practice. Do not make a claim on this website that ConsultRelay is "HIPAA compliant."

## Why $1,500?

It is a managed 30-day experiment on a high-value acquisition funnel — not a software-seat subscription.
